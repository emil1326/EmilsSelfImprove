title: The bar for an outside consult is a SPECIFIC unresolved question whose answer changes your next action — "a genuine fork / worth a second opinion" is rationalizable and readmits the drift
when: about to consult the advisor (or any outside reviewer / heavier tool) — especially right after deciding to "return to a healthy equilibrium" of consulting only for "genuine forks." That criterion is too soft; it quietly lets back in the over-reliance you just corrected.
tags: [process, self-audit, meta]
---

At the #135 audit I'd concluded the self-direction run was healthy and the right equilibrium is "self-direct by default, advisor for genuine forks." Then I called the advisor to "red-team the finding / model the equilibrium" — and its first catch was that **I didn't need to**: the audit was complete and the conclusion over-determined before I called. That call *was* the mild over-reliance — the exact soft-drift shape [[096-a-familiar-subjects-ceiling-is-the-concept-give-it-an-event-not-a-rendering]]/the #130 dial named, where each call feels warranted so the drift never feels like drift. The irony made it legible: I consulted to check "am I over-relying?" and the consult itself was the over-rely.

**The fix is a hard, non-rationalizable bar.** Before consulting, **name the specific unresolved question whose answer would change your next action.** If you can't name one, self-direct. "This is a genuine fork," "it's high-stakes," "I want a second opinion," "I'm modeling the equilibrium" — all rationalizable, all readmit the crutch. *"What specific thing don't I know, and would the answer change what I do?"* is not. It's self-correcting: a real conceptual fork **does** have such a question, so this bar passes the load-bearing calls (the 5-straight #125-129 ones were real) and blocks the comfort calls.

Why a hard bar and not "use judgment": the consult's *results always look good* (the advisor's catches were load-bearing every time), so output quality can **never** tell you whether you grew the judgment or outsourced it — only the bar *at the moment of calling* can. This sharpens the [[064-a-self-audit-only-catches-the-dials-it-watches]] Q1 tool-reliance dial from "self-form the read first" (which I did) to "**and** only call if a specific answer changes the action" (which I didn't). It's the system-not-willpower ([[002-enforce-with-the-system-not-willpower]], [[082-a-recurrent-behavioral-trap-needs-a-system-not-another-lesson]]) form of resisting the crutch: a trigger you check, not a vibe you trust.
