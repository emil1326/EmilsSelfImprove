---
title: Iridescence reads as STRUCTURE only when the angle-dependent hue-SHIFT is visible — base-hue + a sheen alone reads as painted/wet
when: rendering structural / iridescent colour (a morpho wing, a beetle carapace, fish scales, an oil slick, opal, a soap film, a hummingbird gorget, nacre). The instinct is "a saturated base hue + a bright specular sheen" — but that reads as painted plastic or just a wet highlight, not iridescence.
tags: [generative, light, color]
---

For Morpho (#077) my first iridescence pass was a saturated blue gradient + a strong white sheen — and it read as *a painted blue butterfly* (and the sheen blew out to glare). What was missing was the load-bearing cue: the angle-dependent **hue-SHIFT**. Real structural colour changes HUE with viewing angle — a morpho slides cyan→violet; an oil slick runs the whole spectrum; nacre drifts pink→green. The eye reads "iridescent / structural" from that *shift*, not from saturation and not from a highlight. Pass 1 made the hue slide cyan→violet across the wing (two ramps — a cyan one and a violet one — lerped by an angle/position field) and it snapped from "painted" to "structural" in a single change.

The lesson: **iridescence = a visible HUE traverse across the form, driven by a position/angle field; the base hue and the sheen are secondary dressing.** A sheen *alone* says "wet/glossy" ([[040-wet-living-surface-needs-all-light-cues-to-agree]] — a catchlight makes a *wet* surface, not a structural one); the hue-shift is what says "structural colour." Two failure modes I hit and fixed: (1) the sheen blew to pure-white glare → keep it TIGHT and TINTED (blue-white), brightness from a narrow band not a wide wash, or it reads as overexposure, not metal; (2) I'd added concentric `sin` rings as "scale texture" and they read as mechanical contour-lines ([[047-first-render-of-a-natural-thing-is-too-regular]]) → drop them, let a faint fbm mottle carry the life. Corollary on a lit ground: the iridescent colour reads luminous by TONE / saturation contrast, not additive glow ([[022-luminosity-on-bright-is-tone]]), and the hue-shift IS the defining feature you're tempted to under-do ([[035-defining-feature-is-often-the-hard-part]]) — skip it and a morpho is just "a blue thing."
