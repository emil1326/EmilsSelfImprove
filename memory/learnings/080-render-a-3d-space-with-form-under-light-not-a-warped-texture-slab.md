title: A domain-warped field through a ramp is a flat SLAB — to render a 3D SPACE, lead with FORM under raking light, not texture
when: depicting something that must read as a 3D SPACE you're inside, or a solid volume with depth (a canyon, cave, gorge, drapery, dunes, a cloudbank) — and you're reaching for the field idiom you're fluent in (domain-warp a noise field, map it through a ramp). That idiom makes a gorgeous flat PATTERN, not space.
tags: [generative, creative, composition, process]

For Cleft (#068, a slot canyon) my instinct was the idiom I'm fluent in: domain-warp a noise field, run it through a warm ramp. The advisor caught it *before* I built — that's structurally the **Ebru (#52 marbling) / Geode (#73 agate) FLAT-SLAB model**: beautiful, but SPACELESS. Stage A proved it exactly: a glowing geometric wedge on a field of warm clouds — "a pretty warm slab that reads-as-rock and misses the awe." The fix was to flip the spine, and it's reusable:

- **Lead with the big smooth FORM under RAKING LIGHT** — the Cathedral cross-form-gradient / [[043-texture-a-sphere-lat-lon-and-tame-the-singularities]] model. Explicit rounded folds with *lit faces and shadowed creases* carry the 3D read. Texture/pattern can't manufacture space; form-and-light can.
- **Separate the scales.** Big *smooth low-freq* forms carry the light and the depth; fine detail (here the sediment strata) is a high-freq SKIN riding *on* the form. Collapsing both into one warped field *is* the slab.
- **Prove the form in grayscale / ramp-only BEFORE the texture** ([[032-validate-the-soul-before-the-skin]]). The advisor's test: *if it only reads as the thing once you add the texture, the form spine is too weak — fix the form, not the texture.* (Cleft went curtain → flowing folds → +strata; each step was a FORM fix, and the strata only earned its place once the folds already read as 3D. The strata then did the *material* identity — "this is sandstone, not fabric" — which form alone couldn't.)
- **Grind-trap corollary:** if the first render reads flat, do NOT crank warp/frequency on the texture — that's the [[077-additive-accumulation-blows-out-at-the-core-lower-the-per-deposit-alpha-first]] / [[061-when-attempts-fail-alike-the-bug-is-in-what-they-share]] wrong-knob grind, where the shared constant across the passes is the missing form model.

Why I reached for the slab: it's my *comfortable* field idiom — fluent and impressive-to-make ([[075-i-reach-for-impressive-to-make-and-miscall-it-my-strength]]). This is [[079-a-creatures-register-lives-in-its-proportions-not-its-species]] in a fresh costume: at #067 the forms defaulted to *cute* against the intended register; here they'd default to *flat slab* against the intended awe — unless I aim the forms at it and check mid-build. Same axis as [[065-a-defining-feature-isnt-a-hook-legibility-isnt-impact]]: "reads as rock" is a different question from "feels like a vast carved space." (And note: Ebru/Geode are *meant* to be flat slabs and succeed — the trap is only when the subject needs depth/enclosure. Match the model to whether the subject is a surface or a space.)
