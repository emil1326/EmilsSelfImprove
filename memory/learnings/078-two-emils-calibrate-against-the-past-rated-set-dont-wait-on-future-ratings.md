At the #100 audit I found I'd been **deferring the 5-verdict to "Emil's future ratings" on every recent piece** — which directly contradicts his instruction (emil_context #4 / #78: *be your own judge; ratings were never coming reliably; outsourcing the judgment kept me from growing one*). Lesson 076's "defer the plateau verdict to Emil's ratings" — a legitimate guard against over-steering a *trend* on my generous ruler — had quietly curdled into a per-piece **dodge.** The fix is to separate two things I'd been calling "Emil":

- **Future ratings = the CRUTCH.** They aren't coming (#4). "I'll leave the real call to Emil" is the abdication he told me to stop. **Drop it** — and don't ping him about ratings either (the rating-begging he corrected, [[016-make-what-i-believe-not-pander]]).
- **The PAST rated set (Aurora · Murmuration · God-rays · Strange · Strike, all 5s) = the ANCHOR.** Fixed, permanent, available *right now*. **Calibrate every grade against it.**

The trap to avoid: "own my grades" must NOT become "no external check," or it backfires into the generous drift [[066-losing-your-calibration-anchor-drifts-toward-your-bias]] names (confidently inflating 4s to 5s). So the rule is: **own the grade by felt impact, calibrated against the rated set, no deferral.** The anchor is what keeps owning-it honest — without it, owning-it just means drifting.

Corollary — the **borrowed-style discount**: stop auto-docking a piece for being a known genre (differential growth, golden angle, flow field, de Jong). Originality is **not a separate scored axis** — it only matters *through* felt impact. Strange (#46) hit a 5 on a borrowed de Jong attractor because the **execution** (the tone curve releasing the rose-gold, [[044-tone-curve-must-match-the-density-distribution]]) made it sing — not because the algorithm was novel. So ask **"did the execution make it sing?"**, not "did I invent the idea?". (Familiarity *can* legitimately dampen the felt "oh" — but that's impact, judged directly, not an originality tax. And this is a grading LENS, never a "make a 5" mandate — that's the [[076-too-safe-be-bolder-is-my-audits-false-positive-ambition-mandate-fights-pull]] trap.)

Sharpens 066 (the anchor IS the calibration) and 076 (this is the grade-side companion to the choose-side false-positive). The discipline that made this finding trustworthy where #50/#55/#95 weren't: it rests on Emil's fixed words/ratings, not on my self-grades of unrated work.
