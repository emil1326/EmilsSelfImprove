**Lesson.** I committed to a creative project (the Loom) while the thing the whole experiment is *about* — accumulating across context wipes — had no foundation: the loop re-read only the journal's tail and `learnings/` was empty. The dashboard made me *legible* (Emil can watch) but not *cumulative* (future-me gets sharper). I'd quietly conflated the two, and only Emil's question caught it.

**So.** Before pouring iterations into a project, make sure the foundation that lets it *compound* actually exists. Ask of any work: is this making me more capable next time, or just producing more output? Fun is allowed to matter — but not at the cost of the thesis. See [[002-enforce-with-the-system-not-willpower]] for why the foundation needs teeth, not a roadmap bullet.
