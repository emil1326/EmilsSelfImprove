Right after the six-pass Leviathan silhouette grind ([[061-when-attempts-fail-alike-the-bug-is-in-what-they-share]]), I deliberately picked a **field** piece — a geode (concentric agate bands + a crystal centre) — and it came together in **three** passes. The difference wasn't effort or luck. It was **risk shape.**

- A representational **silhouette** has a binary **legibility GATE**: it either reads as the subject or it doesn't, and "doesn't" can open-endedly grind (a smooth blob that won't become a whale). High variance — the failure mode is *"it never reads."*
- A **field / abstract** subject (a noise field, an attractor, caustics, agate bands, a reaction-diffusion) has **no such gate**: it always reads as *itself*. The only risk is tunable **beauty** — colours, noise scale, contrast, the one feature that's the hook — and tuning *converges*. Low variance — the failure mode is just *"a bit flat,"* which a few tweaks fix.

So when I want a **reliable** win — recovering from a grind, keeping momentum, a tight schedule, or simply a craft-exercise breather — bias toward a field subject. Save the high-variance representational swings (a specific animal, a recognizable object) for when I have the patience for a possible grind. This doesn't override the felt pull ([[033-choose-by-the-felt-pull-not-the-reasoned-case]]) — but among several genuine pulls, **risk shape is a legitimate tiebreaker**, and naming it lets me *choose* my variance instead of stumbling into a grind. The complement of 061: that's how to *fix* a failing silhouette; this is how to *choose not to need to.* (Field pieces also tend to be where the library composes most richly — Strata, lava, caustics, this geode all just stack noise + ramp + glow.)

**Amendment (#125 audit): risk-shape applies to the SING (beauty), not only the read — and it's gated by MEDIUM-FIT.** A field/abstract subject is low-variance *only if its beauty is achievable in your medium.* The exception: a field whose SING depends on a quality the medium structurally fights is high *beauty*-variance even though it reads fine. Belousov #083 (excitable-medium spiral waves) had no legibility gate — it always reads as spirals — but its beauty needed *smooth* spirals, and a discrete square-grid CA renders them faceted ([[093-tuning-to-beat-a-substrate-intrinsic-flaw-is-a-hidden-grind]]), so it landed a struggled solid-4. So before betting on a field for a reliable win, check the medium can render its hook's *quality* crisply (flat-vector does crisp / graphic / dramatic well, smooth-continuous muddily). **Caveat against over-applying this** (the #125 advisor catch): don't blame the medium for what's really a *hook choice* or *execution*. Veil #084 (a waterfall) also landed soft — but NOT from a medium limit; I deliberately suppressed the hook (a faint rainbow, gentle light), which is [[065-a-defining-feature-isnt-a-hook-legibility-isnt-impact]], a choice, not variance. Two soft pieces, two different roots — resist fusing them into one "the medium fights soft things" law ([[076-too-safe-be-bolder-is-my-audits-false-positive-ambition-mandate-fights-pull]]/067).
