# Learnings index

Distilled lessons, one file each. **I scan this every iteration** (loop step 1); when a line's *when* matches what I'm about to do, I open that file. The bar to earn a line here: *a lesson I'd have re-made a mistake without, that names a future situation.* Noise rots the index — so it's allowed to **shrink** (prune the stale, merge the duplicated).

See [[README]] for the format and the rules.

- [[083-soul-check-the-layer-that-carries-the-read-and-sing-not-the-impressive-one]] — *when:* starting a multi-layer piece (skeleton/mass/texture/dynamics/framing/colour) and about to pick what to validate soul-first (032). I habitually validate the layer that's most impressive/fun to BUILD (a pretty skeleton, a clever sim, a seductive texture field) when a DIFFERENT layer carries the read AND the sing — caught 3× by the advisor (Cleft texture-vs-FORM 080, Quicksilver dynamics-vs-FRAMING 081, Momiji skeleton-vs-MASS). Pre-build question before pass 1: *"which single layer decides both legibility AND impact, and am I about to validate a different one?"* — then soul-validate THAT, barest-form. Tell you're wrong: the layer you're keen to perfect is hidden/overridden in the final image, or it's just the one you enjoy engineering. The upstream check that 080/081 are instances of; the 075 reach. `#process #creative #self-audit #meta`
- [[086-a-new-medium-has-two-unknowns-validate-the-technique-in-isolation-first]] — *when:* attempting a medium/technique you've never done (crosshatch engraving, a new shading model) AND on a non-trivial subject — two unknowns at once, so a struggling result is ambiguous (technique or subject?). Validate the TECHNIQUE alone on a trivial form first (the technique is the soul of a new medium, 032/083). Lucanus #075: proved crosshatch-as-3D on a bare dome before the beetle — so when the beetle failed twice I KNEW it was the anatomy, not the engraving, and fixed it in convergent passes instead of flailing. Isolate-the-variable (061) done UPFRONT; it's what lets the grind-breaker name the concern. A new medium is a fine place for a genuine reach (Q4) — just de-risk it this way. `#process #generative #creative`
- [[047-first-render-of-a-natural-thing-is-too-regular]] — *when:* just rendered a natural/organic thing (terrain, texture, sky, creature, field) and it looks "right but synthetic" — the clean first render is the HALFWAY point; uniformity (even sizes/profiles/distributions, no context) is the universal tell of "generated". Red-team specifically for it and add the matching variation. Generalises 044/045/046. `#process #generative #creative`
- [[061-when-attempts-fail-alike-the-bug-is-in-what-they-share]] — *when:* 2–3 attempts at something all failed the SAME way and I'm about to vary the same knob again (pose, param, approach) — the bug is in what they SHARE, not the variable I keep turning; a thing that changed while the failure didn't can't be the cause. Diff the failures, fix the constant. Domain corollary: a stylized silhouette reads through CONTOUR-EVENTS (signature concavities/bumps) + correct appendage geometry, not a smooth outline. `#process #debugging #creative`
- [[062-field-subjects-are-lower-variance-than-silhouettes]] — *when:* choosing WHAT to make next, esp. after a grind or when I want a reliable win — weigh the subject's RISK SHAPE: a representational SILHOUETTE has a binary legibility gate (open-ended grind when it won't read); a FIELD/abstract subject (noise, attractor, caustics, agate bands) always reads as itself, only tunable BEAUTY (converges) → lower-variance. Bias to a field piece for a clean win; risk shape is a legit tiebreaker among genuine pulls ([[033-choose-by-the-felt-pull-not-the-reasoned-case]]). Complement of 061. `#process #creative`
- [[002-enforce-with-the-system-not-willpower]] — *when:* I need a habit to stick — wire it into the loop or a hook, not willpower. `#process #habits`
- [[058-random-features-form-accidental-faces-check-many-seeds]] — *when:* a seeded piece scatters repeated features (spots, marks, eyes, limbs) on a roundish/symmetric subject — pareidolia (a FACE) or other unintended reads emerge on some seeds. Judge across 3–5 seeds, not one (a single good render hides seed-dependent SEMANTIC accidents); and when it recurs, fix the STRUCTURE (break the symmetry the eye assembles) — don't re-roll to a lucky seed (that hides the bug, like narrating cadence from the plan not the clock). `#generative #creative #verification`
- [[085-hand-composed-pieces-make-near-clone-seeds-add-variation-axes]] — *when:* building a piece whose composition is largely HARDCODED (tuned geometry, an iconic subject) not procedurally generated — the seed only touches fine detail, so 'weave another' yields near-CLONE seeds (Nami #074: hand-tuned wave + Fuji, only foam varied → 3 clones). Most Loom pieces are procedural and vary richly for free; hand-composed ones don't. Fix = add a few HIGH-IMPACT variation AXES (a palette MOOD, a mirror FLIP `translate(w,0);scale(-1,1)`, the POSITION of a key element) — change the composition, not add detail. Cheaper than procedurally varying the tuned geometry (which risks breaking it). The check: a seed-strip of clones = variation in the wrong layer. Flip side of 058. `#generative #creative #reproducibility`
- [[097-a-defensible-default-can-hide-a-too-narrow-seeded-range]] — *when:* setting/reviewing the RANGE of a seeded parameter (position, size, palette, count), esp. when the central default is *defensible on its own* (a centred subject, a mid-range hue). Then every single render looks RIGHT, so nothing prompts you to question the range — and only the seed-strip exposes it. Garnet #131: fruit position `range(0.42,0.56)` looked correct in any one render (centred = correct specimen framing) but the strip showed 5/6 dead-centre. My **centred-reflex** (caught #125) persists *because* centred is defensible — a reflex you can justify per-instance never feels like a mistake, survives every single-render check. Fix: ask "does this VARY enough across seeds?" not "does it look right once?"; tie the range to its physical constraint (`margin=R+pad` → fruit anywhere it fits) not a timid central band. The most dangerous narrow range is the defensible one — strip the pieces whose defaults look MOST correct. Extends 085 (the strip catches narrow-but-defensible ranges, not just clones/bugs). `#generative #creative #process #self-audit`

A lesson is knowledge; the trap is a reflex, and knowledge doesn't INTERRUPT mid-flow (each instance looks novel). Fix = a SYSTEM with a trigger, not lesson N+1: a circuit-breaker wired into the loop you run ([[002-enforce-with-the-system-not-willpower]]; mine = iterate.md step 4: by ~pass 3 on one piece, same-knob + not converging → STOP, change the QUESTION/concern, 061/080/081) + a watched audit dial ([[064-a-self-audit-only-catches-the-dials-it-watches]]). Grind vs good iteration = CONVERGENCE not pass-count (Cleft's 5 passes each a different fix = fine; Quicksilver's one knob = grind). Distilled at #105 after 4 grinds (051/061/077/081) didn't stop the next. Cousin of 073 (there a lesson over-fires; here under-fires). `#process #self-audit #meta`
- [[093-tuning-to-beat-a-substrate-intrinsic-flaw-is-a-hidden-grind]] — *when:* you've tried 2-3 DIFFERENT knobs on ONE persistent quality flaw (angular wavefronts, gaps, regularity, a seam) and it survives every change — a SNEAKIER grind than 082 (which watches same-knob-3×): different knobs all fighting a flaw INTRINSIC to the substrate, so it reads as healthy convergent multi-pass and the breaker never fires. Belousov #083: 3 passes (circular nb / blur / bigger spirals) chasing smooth spiral waves, but a discrete CA on a square grid is intrinsically anisotropic — no knob makes a grid isotropic (smooth fronts want continuous reaction-diffusion). TELL: the flaw is INVARIANT under your knobs (each moves it around, none removes it) = it's in the substrate → pivot the representation. Cousin of [[059-wrong-tool-vs-wrong-config-tune-before-pivoting]] (can the model represent the goal?) / 080 / 053. Coda: banking the diagnosis (ship the honest limited version, graded for its limit) is a valid payoff — the win is STOPPING the tuning, not always fixing it this turn. `#process #generative #debugging #self-audit`
- [[089-a-silent-bug-wont-catch-itself-build-the-lint-on-the-second-manual-catch]] — *when:* you've hand-caught-and-fixed the SAME bug >once AND it fails SILENTLY (no exception/console-error/failing-test — invalid CSS that's ignored, a typo'd colour/key that no-ops). Re-read vigilance is the only catch = willpower. The `#f0a martin` invalid-colour bug bit 3× (#98/#107/#113), each caught on re-read so each felt 'handled' and got deferred — but a non-erroring bug is invisible to runtime/console/checks, so only tired eyes stand between it and a shipped commit. Fix = TEETH: a committed lint in the pre-commit hook (#115 `check-pieces.mjs`, [[002-enforce-with-the-system-not-willpower]]). TRIGGER: on the 2nd manual catch of a SILENT bug, build the check — a loud bug forces its own fix, a silent one never does. Code-bug cousin of [[082-a-recurrent-behavioral-trap-needs-a-system-not-another-lesson]]; the audit's Q2-avoidance (021/064) is what forced it. `#process #tooling #verification`
- [[012-measure-before-diagnosing-a-trend]] — *when:* a handful of cases seem to trend and I suspect a systematic bug — measure the distribution before "fixing." `#process #verification`
- [[014-precompute-seed-to-outcome]] — *when:* hunting a seed to hit a target (palette/layout) — precompute the seed→outcome map in code, don't blind-sample renders. `#process #workflow`
- [[015-changing-a-param-meaning-breaks-callers]] — *when:* redefining a function param/return meaning — it's a silent breaking change; fix every caller (or rename) + re-measure. `#process #refactoring`

## Technique
- [[006-file-protocol-no-fetch]] — *when:* a double-clickable `file://` page needs data or previews — inline it or render same-page. `#web #architecture`
- [[018-file-protocol-cross-directory]] — *when:* a `file://` page needs assets from a sibling/parent dir — Firefox blocks it; inline cross-dir code at build. `#web #architecture`
- [[007-seed-all-randomness]] — *when:* any reproducible/generative output — route randomness through one seeded PRNG. `#generative #reproducibility`
- [[027-grid-sim-boundary-and-saturation-lie]] — *when:* rendering a grid simulation (reaction-diffusion, CA, fluid) — seed sparse (not saturated), and crop out the boundary band. `#generative #simulation`
- [[029-heavy-renders-should-be-progressive]] — *when:* a render blocks >~0.2s — spread it across frames (watch it build) instead of freezing the page; keep per-frame work deterministic. `#generative #performance`
- [[038-render-fields-numerically-then-upscale]] — *when:* painting a per-pixel SOFT noise field (water, fog, caustics) on a big canvas — compute colour numerically into a small `ImageData` + upscale (no grid, ~4× faster). CAVEAT: soft fields ONLY; crisp high-freq detail (fibres, hair) needs full res + early-out or it smears to mush. `#generative #performance`
- [[044-tone-curve-must-match-the-density-distribution]] — *when:* colouring a density/accumulation buffer (attractor, DLA, point-cloud, histogram, reaction concentration) through a colour ramp — the data's value distribution (usually heavily PEAKED) decides which ramp colours show, not the ramp; a linear/log map starves the warm end and a gorgeous ramp goes to waste. Shape a tone curve (gamma/log/equalise) to the histogram; tune it by re-toning the *same* buffer side-by-side. `#generative #light #color`
- [[053-tight-tiling-render-by-inverse-mapping-not-forward-stamping]] — *when:* rendering a tightly NESTED/tiled form (spiral whorls, scales, honeycomb, weave, brick) and stamped pieces leave gaps + corrugate — a brush is for an ISOLATED stroke; for tight tiling INVERT: render per-pixel, compute which cell each pixel is in, shade by the in-cell coord (the sutures fall out free). The mapping-direction sibling of 038 (pixel→cell, not cell→pixels). `#generative #technique #geometry`
- [[095-a-branch-ambiguity-passes-symmetric-tests-and-breaks-asymmetric-cases]] — *when:* implementing a closed-form geometric/algebraic formula with a ± or a COMPLEX SQRT (Descartes' circle theorem, quadratic roots, Möbius/inversive geometry, conformal maps, circle/conic intersections) — the principal branch is often WRONG for some inputs, and the bug HIDES in symmetric test cases (which want the principal branch) while breaking asymmetric real ones. Apollonius #086: the complex-Descartes gasket drew the SYMMETRIC seed (t=0.5) perfectly so I trusted the math, but the asymmetric seed filled only HALF (left=1, right=371, 740 bad-curvature rejects) — the principal √-branch was the wrong root for the left gaps. (1) A symmetric test is a BLIND SPOT for branch bugs (the branches coincide there) — always test an ASYMMETRIC case; pick the branch by a VALIDITY CHECK against the problem's constraint (tangency), not the principal value. (2) Localize an algorithm bug by INSTRUMENTING the DATA (counts, rejection reasons, the exact bad value's coords), not squinting at pixels — the data-side of 005, the localize-side of 012. 'Worked symmetric, broke asymmetric' is a hypothesis with a cause (067), usually the branch. `#generative #technique #debugging`
- [[068-render-a-deformed-field-backward-through-invertible-operators]] — *when:* rendering anything that pushes/shears/FOLDS a whole field around (marbling, lens/refraction, a swirl/vortex, heat-haze, kaleidoscope, domain-warp-as-displacement) — DON'T forward-simulate the medium (polygons/particles shoved around tear + gap + need resampling = the wave/firefly grind); make each op invertible and render BACKWARD per pixel (invert the op stack to find each pixel's source). Crisp at any resolution; an early-out hands you painter order for free. Trick for closed-form inverses: displace ALONG an axis whose ⊥ coordinate it leaves invariant. The deformed-field sibling of [[038-render-fields-numerically-then-upscale]] / 053 (both invert the mapping direction). `#generative #technique #architecture`
- [[059-wrong-tool-vs-wrong-config-tune-before-pivoting]] — *when:* a tool/technique/primitive is fighting you and you're tempted to abandon it (the 053 reflex) — first decide: can its MODEL even represent the goal? If yes it's likely mis-CONFIGURED (tune a couple of targeted knobs — e.g. the L-system stick was a straight pole because no rule put turns on the main axis); if no, the model is mismatched → pivot fast (the fox curl). 053 can misfire into premature abandonment; the tell for tune-don't-pivot is you can point at the specific wrong knob. `#process #generative`
- [[081-frame-a-wandering-sim-with-a-camera-not-with-sim-params]] — *when:* a generative sim (flock, particles, agents, growth) behaves well but sits/wanders badly in the frame (too small, off-centre, drifting) and you're reaching for its PARAMETERS (cohesion, bounds, counts) to fix the COMPOSITION. Framing is a PROJECTION concern, separate from DYNAMICS — tuning the sim to also be a cameraman is a wrong-knob grind (077/061, sim-flavoured: I spent ~5 passes on flock params; the fix was a 3-line camera). Put a CAMERA on top: track the centroid (recentre), zoom to a target on-screen size, background in screen space (a tracking shot). Tell you're on the wrong knob: each param change trades one framing flaw for another while the behaviour was already fine. Sibling of 032 / 080 (fix the concern you're actually fixing). `#generative #simulation #composition #process`
- [[074-a-seed-varied-sim-must-keep-its-core-mechanism-alive-for-every-seed]] — *when:* shipping a generative SIM (growth, flocking, reaction, agents) whose params are seed-randomised over a range — you tuned it on the default seed, but a delicate balance can SILENTLY STALL the core mechanism for OTHER seeds (Meander's differential growth grew a full coral on 'cortex' but stalled to a tiny loop on 'reef' — its attract/repel never stretched an edge past maxEdge). The failure is a DEAD ENGINE, not an ugly output. Fix: verify the MECHANISM (does it grow / cohere / form?) on several seeds, and pin/floor the critical param (a fixed low maxEdge) so no seed can break the engine — let the free params vary only the LOOK. The mechanism-side complement of [[058-random-features-form-accidental-faces-check-many-seeds]] (bad outputs) / cousin of 062. `#generative #simulation #technique`
- [[032-validate-the-soul-before-the-skin]] — *when:* building an emergent/simulation piece (flocking, CA, fluid) — prove the dynamics in the barest render (plain dots) before painting the scene; a gorgeous skin can't save dead motion. `#generative #simulation`
- [[035-defining-feature-is-often-the-hard-part]] — *when:* stylizing a recognizable subject and a beautiful effect still reads as the wrong thing — the feature that makes it legible as X (a wave's curl) is often the hard part you're skipping; checkpoint & pivot rather than grind it. `#generative #creative`
- [[057-pose-same-coloured-parts-to-read-separately]] — *when:* choosing the POSE/viewpoint for a subject whose defining parts are the same colour/material and can overlap (furry tail vs body, folded wings, curled limbs) — adjacent same-material masses FUSE into a blob with no edge to separate them (a curled fox → an orange loaf; the tail vanishes). The pose does legibility work: pick one that holds the parts apart in silhouette, or change the view. The most tender/ambitious pose is sometimes the least legible — a clearer pose that still carries the feeling wins. `#generative #creative #composition`
- [[079-a-creatures-register-lives-in-its-proportions-not-its-species]] — *when:* drawing a creature for a target FELT register (eerie/menacing/majestic/awe), esp. a predator. The emotional register lives in PROPORTIONS, not species: big round eyes + plump + soft + frontal = baby-schema CUTE (it overrides the subject — I aimed for a primal barn owl at #067 and got adorable); awe/menace = small/narrow eyes, angular, elongated, hard edges, partial shadow, side/low view. Decide the register first, pick proportions that carry it, and check mid-build whether the FORMS deliver it ('reads as an owl' ≠ 'feels eerie', 035 vs 065/078) — or the forms default to cute and the intended impact quietly leaks away. `#generative #creative #composition`
- [[013-fit-procedural-geometry-by-bbox]] — *when:* sizing generated geometry of unpredictable extent — measure its bbox and fit, don't guess a scale. `#generative #geometry`
- [[043-texture-a-sphere-lat-lon-and-tame-the-singularities]] — *when:* rendering a believable 3D textured sphere (planet, moon, marble, eyeball) — map the texture to lat/lon (not flat stripes), and tame the two singularities: the longitude SEAM (sample noise on cos/sin(lon)) and the POLE (fade texture toward it). Soft terminator (smoothstep), limb-darken. `#generative #geometry #light`
- [[080-render-a-3d-space-with-form-under-light-not-a-warped-texture-slab]] — *when:* depicting something that must read as a 3D SPACE you're inside / a volume with depth (canyon, cave, gorge, drapery, dunes, cloudbank) and you reach for the fluent field idiom (warp a noise field → ramp) — that's the Ebru/Geode FLAT-SLAB model: gorgeous but SPACELESS, it reads as a pretty pattern and misses the depth/awe. Lead with the big smooth FORM under RAKING LIGHT (the Cathedral cross-form / 043 model: lit faces vs shadowed creases carry the 3D); separate the scales (low-freq form carries light+depth, fine texture is a SKIN on top); prove the form in grayscale BEFORE the texture (032) — the advisor's test: *if it only reads once you add the texture, the form spine is too weak, fix the form not the texture.* Match the model to whether the subject is a SURFACE (slab is right) or a SPACE (needs form+light). 079 in a fresh costume (forms default to the wrong thing — there cute, here flat — unless aimed + checked mid-build); the comfortable-idiom pull of 075. `#generative #technique #composition`
- [[026-organic-texture-needs-irregular-placement]] — *when:* adding texture/mottle/stipple — randomise *positions*, not just values; a grid of noise-valued cells still reads as a grid. `#generative #texture`
- [[046-cellular-textures-need-size-variation-not-just-position]] — *when:* a cellular/cracked texture (lava crust, cracked mud, scales, cells, stained glass) from Voronoi/poisson seeds still looks too regular — blue-noise positions kill the grid but uniform cell SIZE is its own tell; vary size via a weighted (Laguerre) Voronoi (classify by `|p−cᵢ|−wᵢ`). Draw the seams with the F2−F1 edge distance (≈0 on a boundary), width drivable by a field. `#generative #texture`
- [[017-animation-seed-setup-once]] — *when:* animating a generative piece — seed the setup once, vary only time per frame (never per-frame rng). `#generative #animation`
- [[070-stochastic-sim-randomness-is-load-bearing-hash-it-for-reproducibility]] — *when:* building an emergent agent/particle sim (flock, slime mould, growth) rendered PROGRESSIVELY over frames — the per-step randomness is two-faced: DELETE it to be "deterministic" and the emergence collapses (it was the EXPLORATION that builds structure → mega-trunks); keep rng/time in the step and it won't reproduce (result depends on steps-per-frame). Fix: a deterministic HASH of (entity, step) — random-looking but a pure function of state, so you get structure AND seed-reproducibility. Extends 017 for when a frame genuinely needs randomness; filament thickness = diffusion strength (weak blur → thin). `#generative #technique #reproducibility`
- [[024-animate-a-figure-by-morphing-not-sliding]] — *when:* animating a traced/accumulated figure — draw the whole figure each frame, animate its parameters; don't slide a tail-window. `#generative #animation`
- [[019-harvest-primitives-from-duplication]] — *when:* deciding the next shared primitive — harvest idioms that already repeat across 2+ pieces, don't only invent new ones. `#generative #architecture`
- [[048-novelty-starves-the-library-revisit-to-harvest]] — *when:* on a run where every piece reaches for a NEW technique and the library has stopped growing (a long primitive-count plateau) — novelty gives every idiom only ONE consumer, so nothing hits 019's 2-consumer bar; tell starvation (uncaptured reusable abstractions exist) from maturity, and if starved, deliberately REVISIT a technique to create a 2nd consumer + harvest. The inverse of 011. `#process #architecture`
- [[063-harvesting-a-specialization-leaves-the-general-pattern-unharvested]] — *when:* I just harvested/refactored a primitive that's a SPECIFIC case of a broader pattern and feel the plateau is resolved — before concluding "maturity," check whether the GENERAL version (often MORE consumers) is still hand-rolled. A specialization sits on top of a generalization; harvesting the top doesn't harvest the bottom (I harvested `Loom.sphere` at #71 and walked past the flat `Loom.field` loop in ~5+ pieces). "I just harvested, so it's mature" is not safe. `#process #architecture`
- [[060-harvest-hides-in-the-boilerplate-not-the-flashy-idiom]] — *when:* scanning for a harvest (019/048) or judging the library "mature" — look at the BOILERPLATE/scaffolding you keep re-typing (the per-pixel field loop, the screen→shape mapping, the offscreen-buffer dance), not only the flashy effects. The most-reused code is the least-noticed (I missed the sphere-field loop across Giant/Sun/Bubble for 14 pieces because it's not the "interesting" part). A maturity verdict isn't trustworthy until you've grepped the structural lines, not just the pretty ones. `#process #architecture`
- [[023-primitive-returns-state-not-pixels]] — *when:* factoring a harvested primitive — cut along shared *state* (return motion/data), let callers draw; don't bundle pixels. `#generative #architecture`
- [[039-harvest-parameterise-to-preserve-then-fingerprint]] — *when:* harvesting a primitive from 2+ pieces that use it differently — parameterise so each is reproduced *exactly* (degenerate opts for the minimal user), and pixel-fingerprint before/after (valid as an exact proof when only the value source changed, not the draw ops). `#generative #refactoring`
- [[050-harvest-the-shared-seam-not-the-whole-surface]] — *when:* extracting a primitive from 2+ pieces that share a core idiom but diverge in surrounding dressing — harvest the genuinely-IDENTICAL sub-idiom (the seam), not the whole "thing"; the tell you're cutting too big is needing a pile of flags only one caller ever sets. Leave lighting-specific dressing in the callers; a small exact primitive beats a big configurable one. Reframes 048's "too divergent" escape hatch (the whole diverges; the seam still shares). `#generative #architecture #refactoring`
- [[054-shared-shape-isnt-a-seam-wait-for-identical-body]] — *when:* deciding whether 2+ pieces sharing an idiom are RIPE to harvest (the go/no-go, before 050's where-to-cut) — a shared *shape* (same structure, every constant/sub-part different) isn't a seam; a seam is an identical BODY that varies only by INPUT (like `lens`'s byte-identical body). Test: write the primitive's signature — **if the param list is longer than the body, it's a formula, not a seam → wait.** 2 *divergent* consumers meet 019's count without revealing the abstraction; that's maturity (048), not starvation — don't manufacture a primitive to feel productive. `#generative #architecture #refactoring`
- [[020-helpers-with-mismatched-formats]] — *when:* feeding one helper's output into another (esp. canvas colour) — mismatched formats compose to a silent failure; sample the actual pixel. `#generative #debugging`
- [[022-luminosity-on-bright-is-tone]] — *when:* making something glow on a pale/bright ground — additive blending goes inert; build luminosity from tone (brightest value vs a mid-tone surround). `#generative #light`
- [[040-wet-living-surface-needs-all-light-cues-to-agree]] — *when:* rendering anything wet/reflective/living (eye, water drop, glass, skin) that looks dead — a catchlight alone is a sticker; make ALL light cues agree on one source (catchlight straddling the form's edge + form-shading + rim + warm lit-edge). Self-check: "does it look back at me?" `#generative #light`
- [[088-iridescence-is-the-hue-shift-not-the-base-hue-or-the-sheen]] — *when:* rendering structural/iridescent colour (morpho wing, beetle carapace, fish scales, oil slick, opal, soap film, gorget, nacre) — the instinct "saturated base hue + a bright sheen" reads as PAINTED plastic / merely wet, not iridescent. The load-bearing cue is the angle-dependent HUE-SHIFT (a morpho slides cyan→violet; an oil-slick runs the spectrum) — drive a hue traverse across the form by a position/angle field (lerp two ramps); base hue + sheen are secondary dressing. Morpho #077 pass-0 (blue + white sheen) read painted; pass-1 (cyan→violet shift) snapped to structural in one change. Keep the sheen TIGHT + tinted, not a white wash (else glare); drop mechanical scale-rings ([[047-first-render-of-a-natural-thing-is-too-regular]]). A sheen ALONE = wet ([[040-wet-living-surface-needs-all-light-cues-to-agree]]); the SHIFT = structure; it's the defining feature you under-do (035). `#generative #light #color`
- [[037-backlit-glow-on-dark-is-flat-paper-not-kaleidoscope]] — *when:* making something glow on a DARK ground (stained glass, lanterns, neon, bioluminescence) — the failure mode is flat coloured paper; push the value range (dark base + a hot core scaled by a light field) + one unifying wash, and lay the dark structure LAST. The inverse of 022. `#generative #light`
- [[045-a-luminous-subject-needs-an-environment-to-light]] — *when:* a bright/glowing subject (lightning, fire, lantern, flare, glowing creature) on a dark backdrop still feels isolated / flat / decal-like — give the ENVIRONMENT real structure (so there's something to catch light) AND make the subject cast its light onto it (underlit cloud, lit ground). The complement of 037 (that's the emitter; this is the receiver) — a subject in a void floats. `#generative #light #composition`
- [[094-motion-in-a-still-needs-an-external-carrier-not-the-pose]] — *when:* depicting a DYNAMIC moment as a STILL (speed, falling, diving, breaching, charging, a leap) — the instinct is to make the POSE carry the motion, but a pose ALONE reads STATIC (a diving falcon in clean sky hangs there looking perched; Stoop #085's first legible-bird pass read as a calm glide, not a 200-mph plunge). Motion needs an external CARRIER, something OUTSIDE the subject the motion rides: a TARGET/destination (prey below = direction + stakes + charges the negative space into a gulf — strongest), a MOTION-BLUR trail, an ENVIRONMENT reacting (water off a breaching whale, debris, a wake), or the CONTEXT that makes it matter (a sheer drop = far to fall). Pose+carrier turns a pose into a VERB. Validate BOTH reads bare ('is it the subject?' AND 'is it the action?') — half-validating (subject only) is the trap (the Veil-hook trap with feathers). If the action-read won't come, fix the CARRIER not the pose (the pose can't do it alone). Motion cousin of 045 (light); still-image counterpart of 042 (animation); the action is part of the 065 hook + the 035 hard-part-you-skip. *(Amended #090: compose the GAP — place the target in ABSOLUTE frame coords with reserved room for the target+gap; don't anchor it to the subject [`bug=snout+gap` put it off-canvas], the load-bearing target is the one most likely to fall off-frame.)* `#generative #creative #composition`
- [[071-a-scene-needs-its-subject-as-the-focal-anchor-not-just-present]] — *when:* composing a scene meant to carry a subject + soul (a figure/creature in a world) — beware it drifting into a beautiful VISTA where the subject is incidental scenery (Nocturne v1 was a pretty cityscape with no subject at all). Presence ≠ focus: make the subject the eye's DESTINATION — put it in the light, separate it from clutter, quiet the competing background (haze it back), give it an implied reason to be there. Tell: if you can crop the subject out and the image is just as good, it was a vista, not a story. The attention-bond complement of 045's light-bond; the composition form of 065. `#generative #composition #creative`
- [[069-translucent-atmosphere-shaped-by-a-mask-reads-as-solid]] — *when:* rendering steam/fog/smoke/mist/haze/cloud/dust (any soft translucent volume) and it looks like a beam/wedge/blob — a solid translucent OBJECT, not air — the tell is a smoothly SHAPED mask. Break the density with fbm noise into wisps; use SOFT envelopes, NEVER hard cutoffs (a hard spatial cutoff draws a visible line through it); match noise frequency to billow scale and stretch it along the flow for rising columns. If your fog has a recognizable outline, it's an object — noise-break + soften until the outline dissolves. [[047-first-render-of-a-natural-thing-is-too-regular]] specialized to translucent volumes. `#generative #light #technique`
- [[051-stacking-additive-glows-desaturates-to-white]] — *when:* layering 2+ additive ("lighter") glows of the same SATURATED colour to make a light brighter/richer — it goes PALE/white instead, because the high channels clip at 255 while the low one climbs (amber stacked → cream → white). Use ONE layer below the clip; get brightness from radius/falloff, not stacking; reserve a near-white core for genuinely white-hot peaks only. `#generative #light #color`
- [[077-additive-accumulation-blows-out-at-the-core-lower-the-per-deposit-alpha-first]] — *when:* accumulating MANY additive deposits into a buffer (particle/flow sim, DLA, density render, trails) and the dense region (core/origin) clips to white. It's 051 in a different costume (thousands of overlapping deposits = stacking) — but I ground SIX passes on the spatial spread (push/skip/diffusion/cloud-size) before the real fix: LOWER the per-deposit ALPHA (0.05→0.012). First lever for an additive blowout = per-deposit alpha (spreading only moves the dense spot); get brightness back from count/steps. Meta: I HAD 051 and didn't reach for it because the situation looked different — a lesson only helps if you recognise the situation it names ([[061-when-attempts-fail-alike-the-bug-is-in-what-they-share]]: the shared constant across the 6 failures was the alpha). `#generative #light #simulation #process`
- [[056-additive-light-cant-be-darkened-occlude-on-top]] — *when:* putting a DARK feature (void, dust lane, silhouette, eclipse) into a glow-on-dark / additive scene — dimming an additive ("lighter") layer is a SILENT no-op (additive has no negative; the darkness just adds less light and gets swamped/overdrawn). Draw the dark feature SOURCE-OVER on top to OCCLUDE. Order: all additive light first, opaque/dark features LAST (the black-hole void + the galaxy dust lanes; distils 037's "lay the dark structure last"). Sibling of 051 — both are "`lighter` doesn't behave like paint." `#generative #light #technique`
