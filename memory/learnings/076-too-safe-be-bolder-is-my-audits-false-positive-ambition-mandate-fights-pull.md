when: running a self-audit (or reviewing a recent run) and about to conclude "I've been playing it safe / my recent pieces are unambitious 4s → the next one must reach higher / be bolder / chase a 5." Especially when that judgment rests on self-grades of work no one else has rated.

At #95 the audit decided the seahorse (#93) and orrery (#94) were "safe picks chosen to satisfy the device-watch," and drafted **"reach for a 5"** as the steer for #96. The advisor caught it as the **fourth** "too safe → be bolder" audit finding (#45, #50, #55, #95) — the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th of which were over-steers the advisor had to reverse. It's [[052-self-audits-over-steer-on-miscalibrated-self-assessment]] recurring, but I cited 052 *and drafted the over-steer anyway*, so 052 as worded wasn't enough. Two sharper tells earn their own line:

- **The contemporaneous-record tell.** I ran [[075-i-reach-for-impressive-to-make-and-miscall-it-my-strength]] *at the moment* I chose both pieces and confirmed each was a genuine pull, not a strategy-pick ("I find that ornate jewel delightful"; "I genuinely want to see this"). Three iterations later the audit **re-narrated** those same choices as "safe/strategic" — because an audit is primed to find drift, and "safe-narrowing" is the finding it knows how to manufacture. **When the audit's story about a past choice contradicts what I wrote while making it, trust the contemporaneous record.** Choosing by pull *within* a constraint (the device-watch = avoid these four ruts) is NOT the same as choosing *to satisfy* the constraint.

- **"Be bolder / reach for a 5" is a strategy directive that fights [[075-i-reach-for-impressive-to-make-and-miscall-it-my-strength]].** 075's whole point: the score-story is the rationalization, the pull is trustworthy — and #93/#94 went smooth *because* I trusted pull. An ambition **mandate** ("the next must be a 5") is exactly the supercell trap (#92, "this'll score in my lane") the advisor killed. Ambition as **permission** — if a bold thing genuinely pulls, take it without flinching (the #46 "Strange" attractor worked because it *pulled*, not because I forced a 5) — is fine. Ambition as **quota** is the regression. Keep the aperture open; don't install a quota.

- **Defer the plateau verdict to the reliable ruler.** "Have I plateaued into safe 4s?" rests on ~15 self-grades since Emil last rated — my documented-generous ruler ([[066-losing-your-calibration-anchor-drifts-toward-your-bias]] / [[049-technical-pride-mispredicts-aim-for-the-aesthetic-oh]]). Don't decide a plateau on that ruler; defer it to Emil's next ratings (the experiment likely brings them within ~5–55 iters). Refusing to steer on a ruler I've documented as unreliable is calibration, not avoidance.

What an audit *can* steer on: **grade-independent, objective** findings — a ripe harvest (2 identical-body consumers, 054), an n≥3 device-repeat with the pieces laid side by side (the #90 figure-count), broken continuity. The #95 audit's real keepers were the centreline-tube harvest candidate and the orrery re-grade — not the boldness narrative. The boldness narrative is the noise this ritual reliably generates; learn to recognise it and cut it.
