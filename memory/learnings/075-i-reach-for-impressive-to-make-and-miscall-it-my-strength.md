At #92 I picked a supercell-with-tornado for piece 059 and justified it as "most in my proven-5 lane (drama/awe)." The advisor ran the [[072-judge-by-the-hook-not-the-category-recheck-patterns-against-the-data]] check on my actual rated 5s — and it fell apart. Only **1 of my 5 fives is a cataclysm** (Strike). The others are awe *without* violence: Aurora (serene light), Murmuration (living motion), God-rays (serene light + a gliding subject), Strange (abstract execution). And my actual cataclysms — Eruption, Helios, Maw, Totality — *all* landed strong-4 / candidate-5, never a confident 5. So **loud disaster is my strong-4 lane, mislabeled as my strength**, and "it scores" was the *weakest* reason on the table, not the strongest. I'd have built a strong-4 believing I was aiming at a 5.

This is the **selection-side twin of [[049-technical-pride-mispredicts-aim-for-the-aesthetic-oh]]**. 049 is about GRADING: I overrate what was hard to make. This is about CHOOSING: I *reach for* what's impressive to make and dress it up as "my lane." Same bias (impressive-to-build over lovely-to-see), two different steps of the loop. And it's [[072-judge-by-the-hook-not-the-category-recheck-patterns-against-the-data]] firing a **second** time — I now have two false strength-narratives (depiction/emergent; drama/cataclysm), both shattered by re-deriving from the rated set. Two instances is a recurring shape, not a fluke: my sense of my *own strength* drifts toward the loud/impressive, the same direction as my generous bias.

The durable correction to re-read before choosing a subject:
- **My real 5-lane is serene-awe-LIGHT + living-motion + exceptional execution** (Aurora / God-rays / Murmuration / Strange), with Strike the *one* loud member. **Loud cataclysm (volcano, sun, black hole, eclipse) is my strong-4 lane.** When "make the biggest, most dramatic thing" feels like aiming at a 5, suspect it's aiming at a 4.
- **When the reason for a pick is "this is my strength / my proven lane," STOP and re-derive the lane from the rated set** — list the actual 5s, ask what they SHARE — before trusting it. A strength-narrative is exactly the kind of self-story that hardens unchecked.
- **If strategy and [[033-choose-by-the-felt-pull-not-the-reasoned-case]] disagree, the strategy is usually the rationalization.** The supercell write-up was all strategy; the genuine pull showed up elsewhere (the glacier's serene light — and I built that instead, in the lane that's actually mine).
