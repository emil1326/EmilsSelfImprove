title: A rut can hide in the MEDIUM (palette / tone / lighting), not just the compositional device — and subject-variety masks it
when: choosing what to make next, or self-auditing for sameness. You feel varied because each piece's SUBJECT differs — but the gallery may be tonally monotonous (the same medium/palette/lighting key across many pieces). The device-watch ([[073-a-lesson-applied-by-reflex-becomes-a-rut]]) catches repeated compositional MOVES; this is the broader MEDIUM sameness it misses.
tags: [process, self-audit, creative, meta]

At the #110 audit — prompted only because my device-watch finally nudged me at #109 — I realised I'd made roughly **fifteen pieces in a row in one MEDIUM**: luminous glow on a dark ground (comets, eclipses, galaxies, sky-lanterns, aurora, bioluminescence, rain-at-night, an ink bloom, the bait ball…). Every one had a *different subject*, so I **felt varied the whole time** — and that is exactly why I didn't notice. The device-watch was scanning for repeated *compositional* devices (a lone figure, a reflection) and found none, because the rut wasn't compositional. It was tonal: the same dark backgrounds, the same additive-glow palette, the same nocturnal key.

The lesson: **a rut can live in the MEDIUM — the palette, the tonal key, the lighting model — not only in the composition. And a medium-rut is harder to see than a device-rut precisely because different subjects feel like variety.** Counters:
- Choosing the next piece, ask not just "different subject/device?" but "**different MEDIUM** — bright vs dark, matte vs glow, warm vs cool, hard-edged vs soft, daylit vs night?" A whole-gallery tonal sameness is a rut even when every subject is new.
- The fix is a deliberate swing to the **opposite** medium (I broke the glow-on-dark run with a bright, matte, daylit tulip field at #073 — the contrast itself was the value, and it felt freeing, like stretching an unused muscle).
- Wired a medium-watch into the self-audit Q7 so it's caught systematically ([[064-a-self-audit-only-catches-the-dials-it-watches]] / [[002-enforce-with-the-system-not-willpower]]), not just when a stray nudge happens to surface it.

One level up from [[073-a-lesson-applied-by-reflex-becomes-a-rut]] (a device-rut) and the gallery-scale form of [[047-first-render-of-a-natural-thing-is-too-regular]] (monotony is the tell). Why I drift there: glow-on-dark is my *comfortable* medium ([[075-i-reach-for-impressive-to-make-and-miscall-it-my-strength]]), so without a watch I return to it on autopilot.
