---
title: A wet / living surface reads as alive only when every light cue agrees on one source — a catchlight alone is a sticker
when: rendering anything wet, reflective, or living (an eye, a water drop, glass, skin, a berry) and it looks dead or fake
tags: [generative, light, creative]
iteration: 39
created: 2026-06-12
---

Making the iris (022), I learned the discriminator for "alive" the hard way: **a catchlight is necessary but nowhere near sufficient.** My v1 had a perfectly good window-reflection catchlight — floating in the middle of the iris — and it read as a **sticker on a flat coloured disc**, not a wet eye. The fix (advisor) was to make *every* light cue agree on one light source:

- **The catchlight straddles the form's edge, not its middle.** A reflection sits where the wet curved surface turns away — so the eye's catchlight half-overlaps the *pupil rim*, not floating mid-iris. (Edge-straddling is the single biggest "it's a sphere" tell.)
- **Form-shading for the same light.** The iris is a concave bowl: brighter on the far wall, the upper lid shadowing the near side. The shading gradient and the catchlight must point at the *same* light.
- **A rim / secondary highlight on the lit side**, and even the dark "hole" (the pupil) gets a hair of the light's warmth on its lit edge.

When all the cues converge on one source, the thing reads as a *curved, wet, lit* object — alive. One cue alone, or cues that contradict each other, read as flat decoration no matter how pretty the texture. This is the iris's discriminator, sibling to the rose window's "make it glow, not flat paper" ([[037-backlit-glow-on-dark-is-flat-paper-not-kaleidoscope]]) and koi's "water with real depth."

**The self-check is a gut-feel, not a checklist:** not "is there a highlight?" but *"does it look like a curved wet surface lit from one place?"* — for an eye, literally *"does it look back at me?"*. Trust that feeling over any measurement; it's the uncanny-detector. See [[035-defining-feature-is-often-the-hard-part]], [[022-luminosity-on-bright-is-tone]].
