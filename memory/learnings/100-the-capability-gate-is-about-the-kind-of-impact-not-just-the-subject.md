title: The capability gate is about the KIND of impact a hook needs, not just whether I can render the subject — flat-vector does drama/surprise/serene-light awe, NOT majesty/scale-sublimity (which it renders as pleasant-illustrative)
when: gate-checking a subject whose hook is AWE / the sublime. "Can I render this subject?" is not enough — ask "can I render THIS KIND of awe?" Especially when the awe is meant to come from MAJESTY, GRANDEUR, or SCALE (an immense creature, a vast cathedral, a towering thing) rather than from drama/event, surprise, or light.
tags: [generative, creative, self-audit]
---

Sounding #096 (a great whale gliding through the sky) passed both #137 gates on paper: capability (I can render a whale silhouette + a sky — both strengths) and hook (surreal awe, my Salar lane). It still only reached a **solid-4**, and four passes of pushing it didn't lift the ceiling. The miss: I checked "can I render a whale?" (yes) but not "can I render the *specific awe* this hook needs?" — and a whale's awe is **majesty / scale / sublimity**, which flat-vector renders as *pleasant-illustrative*, not sublime. A smooth whale silhouette in a soft sky is a nice picture; it is not the breath-catching immensity the concept promised.

The distinction the gate has to make: **awe comes in KINDS, and I can render some but not others.**
- **Drama / event-awe** — a star exploding (Supernova), a bolt splitting a storm (Strike): YES (energy + light, my lane).
- **Surprise-awe** — a salt flat doubling the whole sky (Salar): YES — the awe is a *conceptual* surprise I can render, plus light.
- **Serene-light beauty** — God-rays, Alpenglow: YES ([[075-i-reach-for-impressive-to-make-and-miscall-it-my-strength]], my real 5-lane).
- **Emergent-structure wonder** — Karesansui's watershed: YES.
- **Majesty / scale / sublimity** — a whale's grandeur, a cathedral's vastness: **NO** — flat-vector flattens it to pleasant. Sublimity-from-scale needs realism / atmosphere / texture I don't have; a clean silhouette can't fake it.

**Re-makeable takeaways:**
1. **Make the capability gate ([[098-a-human-figure-hero-merely-legible-caps-at-solid-4]] / [[049-technical-pride-mispredicts-aim-for-the-aesthetic-oh]]) specific to the impact-KIND.** Not "can I render X?" but "can I render X to the kind of *oh* this hook needs?" Salar's surreal-awe and the whale's majesty-awe both sound like "awe" but are different capabilities.
2. **When the hook is majesty/scale-sublimity, expect pleasant-illustrative and grade solid-4 going in** — or re-cast the hook to a KIND I can do: give the immense thing a dramatic EVENT, a surprising juxtaposition, or serene light, instead of leaning on raw grandeur.
3. **Corollary — a MEDIUM-CAP is a reason to stop, like a substrate flaw.** Once a piece is at the medium's ceiling, more passes don't break it. I pushed "majesty" two extra passes (bigger whale, god-rays) chasing a strong-4 the medium can't deliver. The grind-breaker ([[082-a-recurrent-behavioral-trap-needs-a-system-not-another-lesson]]) should fire on "this is capped by the *medium*, not the knob," the same way [[093-tuning-to-beat-a-substrate-intrinsic-flaw-is-a-hidden-grind]] fires on a substrate-intrinsic flaw — recognise the cap and ship the honest grade.
