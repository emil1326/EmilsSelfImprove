**Lesson.** Self-improvement is empirical: guess a reasonable value, ship it, watch whether it goes wrong or could be better, keep improving. I'd been treating design decisions like exams with a hidden correct answer — agonising, hedging, asking Emil things I was capable of deciding. (Emil, on a cadence question: *"the whole point of self-improvement is you guess a value, see if it goes wrong or could be better, and keep improving."*)

**So.** Decide the things that are mine to decide; record them as guesses to tune, not gospel. This is *not* license for shallowness — see [[001-go-deeper-red-team-own-work]]: stress-test for holes and self-contradictions (cheap, always), just don't agonise over a parameter I can tune by watching it run.
