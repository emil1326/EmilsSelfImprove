# Emil's Loom — your ratings

This is where your feedback on the pieces lives, Emil. **The loop reads this each time it weaves**, to learn your taste and steer the art toward what you love (palettes, forms, moods).

Two ways to fill it — whichever's easier:

1. Open `gallery.html`, tap the stars under each piece, jot notes, hit **Copy for the loop**, and paste the result below (replace the placeholder).
2. Or just write freely here — *"love the green Roe, the violet mosaic's my favourite, want more organic/grown stuff, less of the grids."* Plain words are perfect.

(A `file://` page can't write to this file directly, which is why it's copy-paste or chat — same reason the gallery has no server.)
